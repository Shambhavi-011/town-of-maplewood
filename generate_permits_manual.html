Chapter 1: The New Jersey Uniform Construction Code (UCC)

The Township of Maplewood Construction Division enforces the New Jersey Uniform Construction Code (N.J.A.C. 5:23). The UCC establishes uniform structural, fire, electrical, plumbing, and mechanical safety standards for all residential and commercial buildings within the municipality.

When is a Municipal Construction Permit Mandatory?

Pursuant to state statute, property owners or licensed contractors must obtain an approved Construction Permit before beginning any of the following scopes of work:

Structural & Building Additions
  • Constructing new homes, room additions, or dormers
  • Interior alterations, wall removals, finishing basements/attics
  • New kitchen or bathroom layout reconfigurations
  • Constructing or replacing outdoor decks or porches
  • Roof replacements and whole-house siding installations
  • Installing sheds or accessory structures > 100 sq ft
  • Demolition of primary dwellings, sheds, or garages
Mechanical, Electrical & Utilities
  • New heating systems, furnaces, boilers, heat pumps
  • Central air conditioning installations or replacements
  • Water heater replacements (tank or tankless)
  • Electrical service panel upgrades (100A to 200A+)
  • Whole-house backup generators and solar panel arrays
  • Gas line extensions, conversions, or fireplace inserts
  • Automatic lawn sprinkler systems (backflow preventer)

Application Submission Methods: GovPilot & Town Hall Drop Box

Applications may be submitted electronically via the Maplewood Online Application Forms portal (GovPilot) on maplewoodnj.gov. Paper UCC application jackets and subcode technical sections (Building, Electric, Plumbing, Fire) can also be submitted in person or placed into the 24/7 Secure Permit Drop Box located at the Park Road side entrance of Maplewood Town Hall.

MANDATORY DIGITAL INSPECTION REQUEST PROCESS:
All inspection requests must be submitted online using the Township’s digital Inspection Request Form on maplewoodnj.gov. Inspections are scheduled within statutory timeframes upon completion of each construction stage: rough framing, rough electric, rough plumbing, insulation, and final approvals. Work must not be covered prior to subcode inspector sign-off.

Chapter 3: Specialized Permits: Forestry, Dumpsters & Engineering

Private Tree Removal Permits (Township Code Chapter 227)

The Township strictly regulates tree removals on private property to preserve its suburban forest canopy. A formal permit is mandatory before removing any tree measuring 12 inches or more in caliper (DBH).

Tree Removal Mandatory Document Checklist
  • Completed Tree Permit Form: Species, DBH caliper, condition, and location.
  • Registered Contractor Verification: Selected from official Registered Tree List.
  • Contractor Insurance: COI showing min. $1,000,000 liability naming Township.
  • Certified Mail Receipts: Postmarked slips sent to all abutting neighbors within 200 ft.
  • Mitigation Plan: 1:1 replacement plan or proof of $375 Tree Fund deposit.
Mandatory Replacement or $375 Contribution

For every permitted tree removed, the applicant must satisfy one statutory mitigation condition:

  • Option A (1-to-1 Native Replanting): Plant an approved native shade tree (min. 2.5-inch caliper) on property within 6 months.
  • Option B (Municipal Tree Trust Fund): Pay replacement contribution of $375.00 per tree into Municipal Tree Bank.

Note: Certified mailings to abutting neighbors are required regardless of tree health.

Public Street Dumpsters & Temporary Storage Containers (PODs)

Placing roll-off debris containers, Bagsters, or portable storage units (PODs) on municipal property requires strict compliance with Chapter 257:

Private Driveway Placement
NO PERMIT REQUIRED

If the container is positioned entirely on private property (inside driveway) without encroaching on public sidewalk or roadway, no permit or fee is required.

Public Street Placement
MANDATORY $50 PERMIT

Placing a container in roadway requires DPW & Police Traffic approval. Maximum duration: 7 calendar days (1 renewal permitted).

STREET DUMPSTER REQUIRED SUBMISSION DOCUMENTS:
  • Completed DPW Container Application (`DPW-CT-01`) filed at least 3 business days in advance.
  • Certificate of Insurance (COI) naming Township of Maplewood as additional insured ($300,000 liability).
  • Flat fee of $50.00 per 7-day period. Reflective markers & amber flashers mandatory. Prohibited on snow routes.

Chapter 4: 311 Citizen Municipal Service Requests — Pothole Intake

Section 4.1: Roadway Defect Intake Standards & Mandatory 7-Point Pothole Data Attributes

When submitting a service request for a pothole or roadway defect via the 311 Citizen Portal, mobile app, or dispatch desk, the DPW Road Maintenance Division requires seven (7) specific data points to triage, dispatch, and track the repair to completion:

Required Field Operational Description & Specific Details System Validation Rule
1. Exact Incident Location Specific house address (e.g., 148 Valley Street) or primary roadway with nearest cross-streets (e.g., Baker St between Maplewood Ave and Ridgewood Rd). Matches municipal GIS address database.
2. Direction & Lane Position Compass heading (Northbound, Southbound, Eastbound, Westbound) and exact lane: center travel lane, tire path, curb lane/gutter, or pedestrian crosswalk. Identifies lane closure safety requirements.
3. Size & Severity Bracket Citizen selects from three standard severity brackets:
• Tier 1 (<25 cm / <10″): Shallow pitting / surface spall (<1″ deep).
• Tier 2 (25–60 cm / 10–24″): Medium pothole, depth 1–3″.
• Tier 3 (>60 cm / >24″): Large crater, depth >3″ with exposed sub-base. 		Tier 3 triggers immediate High Priority Dispatch Flag.
4. Jurisdiction Validation Verification whether local municipal road or Essex County roadway (e.g., Springfield Ave, Valley St, Wyoming Ave). County roads automatically routed to County DPW with cross-reference tracking. Prevents misplaced work orders.
5. Environmental & Hazards Hazard flags: Standing water concealing hole depth, sharp jagged asphalt edges posing blowout risk, or encroachment on bicycle lanes or school crosswalks. Water-filled cavities escalated to emergency triage.
6. Digital Photographs Two (2) images: (1) Wide shot showing roadway location context, and (2) close-up shot showing cavity depth and pavement deterioration. Geo-tag coordinates verified against location pin.
7. Citizen Contact Info Reporter full name, telephone number (for SMS dispatch alerts), and email address. Generates automated tracking ID (CASE-2026-XXXX). Anonymous reports accepted without live tracking.

Operational Response SLAs & Duplicate Suppression

Pothole Response SLAs

Tier 3 / Travel Lane Craters: Dispatched within 24 hours for emergency cold/hot-patch asphalt compaction.

Tier 1 & Tier 2 / Secondary Roads: Inspected and repaired within 3 to 5 business days during scheduled ward sweep.

Automated Duplicate Suppression Rule

Submissions within a 50-meter geographic radius logged within the prior 30 calendar days are automatically consolidated into the active parent ticket.

New reporters are appended as subscribed citizens receiving real-time SMS updates without creating redundant crew dispatches.

The 4-Stage Municipal Work Order Status Lifecycle

Every service request logged into the 311 system follows a transparent, four-stage lifecycle with real-time notifications dispatched via SMS or email:

Stage 1: Received (Status: New)

Request ingested into central database. GIS verification and duplicate check executed. Unique tracking ID (CASE-2026-XXXX) dispatched to citizen within 2 minutes.

Stage 2: Dispatched (Status: Assigned)

Work order reviewed by area maintenance supervisor and assigned to specific road crew, vacuum truck, or tree team. Route optimization scheduled by severity bracket.

Stage 3: In Progress (Status: Active)

Field crew actively on site. Safety perimeter established, asphalt compacted, or limb cleared. If secondary utility work required, ticket updated with sub-status.

Stage 4: Completed (Status: Closed)

Work inspected and approved by field foreman. Timestamped completion photo uploaded to ticket record. Citizen receives automated SMS/email resolution notice.

HOW CITIZENS CAN TRACK REAL-TIME 311 TICKETS:
Check live work order status 24/7 by visiting maplewoodnj.gov/services/track and entering your CASE-2026-XXXX number, or by texting STATUS [CASE-NUMBER] to (555) 311-MAPL.

Chapter 6: Prior Approvals, Commercial Business Licensing & Master Fee Schedule

Starting a Business in Maplewood: Licensing, Zoning Verification & Concierge Support

Under Township Code Chapter 142, all commercial enterprises, food establishments, retail stores, personal services, and home-based occupations must obtain a Municipal Business Licence ($75.00–$250.00 annual) prior to commencing operations.

Location Zoning Verification (Before Lease Signing)

• Zoning Check: Entrepreneurs must confirm their proposed commercial address is zoned for the intended use by consulting the Official Zoning Map (C-1 Historic Downtown, C-2 Highway Commercial, M-1 Light Industrial).
• Zoning Certificate of Occupancy: Property owners and prospective tenants must submit a Zoning Review Application ($75.00) to certify that the proposed use is permitted as-of-right. Uses requiring variances are referred to the Zoning Board of Adjustment.

Economic Development Concierge Roundtable

• Concierge Desk: The Planning Division (planning@maplewoodnj.gov / (973) 762-8120 Ext. 3300) provides end-to-end guidance through the municipal approval process.
• Free Pre-Consultation Roundtable: Schedule a 30-minute roundtable with Building, Planning, Health, and Fire officials to review floor plans, change-of-use UCC permits, grease traps, hood fire suppression, and ADA barrier-free access before filing formal permits.

Chapter 8: Mandatory Inspection Milestones & Department Directory

The 5 Mandatory Construction Inspection Milestones

Under N.J.A.C. 5:23-2.18, work must not proceed beyond designated milestone stages without documented sign-off from licensed municipal subcode officials. Work covered prior to inspection is subject to mandatory stop-work orders and physical opening:

1. Bottom of Footing Inspection

Conducted prior to pouring concrete. Verifies excavation depth (min. 36″ frost depth; 42″ for decks), soil bearing integrity, pier hole dimensions, and rebar positioning.

2. Foundation & Underground Utilities

Conducted prior to backfilling. Verifies wall parging, waterproofing, perimeter footing drains, sub-slab plumbing lines under pressure test, and anchor bolts.

3. Rough Framing, Plumbing & Electrical

Conducted after full structural frame, roof sheathing, rough wiring, and rough piping are installed. Must be approved BEFORE insulation or wallboard is hung.

4. Insulation & Thermal Envelope

Verifies R-value compliance (R-21 walls, R-49 attics), continuous vapor barriers, air-sealing of penetrations, and proper baffles at roof soffits prior to drywall installation.

5. Final Inspections & Certificate of Occupancy / Approval (CO / CA)

Conducted upon 100% completion of all construction, electrical, plumbing, mechanical, and fire protection scopes. Once all subcode inspectors sign the permit jacket, the Construction Official issues the formal Certificate of Occupancy (CO) or Certificate of Approval (CA). Occupancy prior to CO issuance carries fines of up to $2,000 per week.
