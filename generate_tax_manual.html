Chapter 1: The Maplewood Property Assessment & Mill Rate Framework

The Township of Maplewood Department of Finance and Office of the Tax Assessor administer the valuation of all real estate within municipal borders in accordance with the New Jersey Constitution (Uniform Assessment Clause) and N.J.S.A. 54:4 et seq.

How Property Taxes Are Determined

A property's total municipal tax levy is calculated by multiplying its official certified assessed valuation by the combined municipal, county, and school tax rate. For the 2026 fiscal year, the Township Committee certified the combined residential tax rate at:

2026 Certified Residential Mill Rate
0.0094523

Equivalent to $0.94523 per $100 of assessed property value, or $9.4523 per $1,000 of assessment.

Statutory Tax Revenue Allocation

• Local School District (48%): Public K–12 education.
• Municipal Government (34%): Police, fire, DPW, roads, parks.
• Essex County Administration (18%): County roads and open space.

Assessment vs. Market Value Equalization

Maplewood conducts continuous sales-ratio analyses supervised by the Essex County Board of Taxation. A property's assessed value reflects its true market value as established during the last municipal-wide revaluation, updated annually by the Essex County Equalization Ratio.

ANNUAL POSTCARD ASSESSMENT NOTICE: On or before February 1 of each tax year, every property owner receives a state-mandated assessment postcard (Form Notice of Assessment). This card reflects the current taxable assessment of the land, building improvements, and total assessment.

Chapter 2: Property Tax Due Dates & Bi-Monthly Billing Schedule

A frequent question from property owners and prospective homebuyers is: "When is the property tax bill due?" In Maplewood, municipal property taxes are billed and due in scheduled instalments every 2 months (bi-monthly cycle) or quarterly in accordance with state statute.

Instalment Period Statutory Due Date Grace Period Expiration Billing Description & Status
Period 1 (Jan/Feb) February 1 February 10 Interim Billing (Based on prior year levy)
Period 2 (Mar/Apr) April 1 April 10 Interim Billing (Spring instalment)
Period 3 (May/Jun) June 1 June 10 Final Billing (Reconciled with municipal budget)
Period 4 (Jul/Aug) August 1 August 10 Final Billing (Summer instalment)
Period 5 (Sep/Oct) October 1 October 10 Final Billing (Fall instalment)
Period 6 (Nov/Dec) December 1 December 10 Final Reconciliation & Year-End Closing

The Statutory 10-Day Grace Period

The Township provides a statutory 10-calendar-day grace period. Tax payments must be received in the Tax Collector's office on or before the 10th of the due month. If the 10th falls on a weekend or municipal holiday, payments are accepted on the next business day without penalty.

STRICT POSTMARK WARNING (N.J.S.A. 54:4-67):
Under New Jersey statutory law, POSTMARKS ARE NOT ACCEPTED as proof of timely payment. Payments must be physically received by the Tax Collector or deposited into the Town Hall 24/7 Drop Box before 4:30 PM on the grace period cutoff date.

Delinquency Interest & Penalties

If payment is not received by the 10th day of the due month, interest is charged retroactive to the original due date at statutory rates:
• 8% per annum on the first $1,500.00 of delinquency.
• 18% per annum on any amount in excess of $1,500.00.
• A 6% year-end penalty is charged on any delinquent balance in excess of $10,000 remaining unpaid on December 31.

Chapter 4: Authorized Payment Methods & Senior / Veteran Tax Relief

The Township provides four secure payment channels for paying property tax bills:

Online Banking & Web Portal

Pay via your financial institution's online bill payment service (Payee: Township of Maplewood - Property Taxes). Enter your 19-digit Tax Account / Roll Number. Payments may also be made on maplewoodnj.gov via e-check ($1.95 fee) or credit card (2.95% fee).

Pre-Authorized Automatic Debit (ACH)

Enroll in Maplewood's direct debit program. Tax payments are automatically deducted from your checking or savings account on each scheduled due date with zero processing fees. Contact the Tax Collector for authorization forms.

In-Person Cashier & 24/7 Town Hall Secure Drop Box

Payments can be delivered in person at the Tax Collector's cashier window at Maplewood Town Hall (574 Valley Street) during business hours (Mon–Fri 8:30 AM – 4:30 PM). A 24/7 Secure Drop Box is located at the Park Road side entrance of Town Hall. Envelopes deposited before 4:30 PM on grace period cutoff dates are credited same day.

State & Municipal Property Tax Relief Programs

Relief Program Eligibility Criteria Benefit Description
Senior Freeze (Property Tax Reimbursement) Age 65+ or receiving SSDI benefits; continuous NJ residency for 10+ years; household income under state limits. Reimburses the difference between baseline year property tax and current year bill.
ANCHOR Program (Affordable Communities) Homeowners and tenants residing in Maplewood on October 1; gross household income under $250,000. Direct state tax relief credit up to $1,750 applied directly or mailed via state check.

Chapter 5: Assessment Appeals, Delinquencies & Tax Directory

How to File an Assessment Appeal

Property owners who believe their property is assessed unfairly relative to true market value have the statutory right to file an appeal with the Essex County Board of Taxation.

ANNUAL FILING DEADLINE: APRIL 1
Under N.J.S.A. 54:3-21, appeals must be received by the Essex County Tax Board on or before April 1 of the tax year. Appeals filed after April 1 cannot be heard by law. If the total assessed value exceeds $1,000,000, direct appeal to the New Jersey State Tax Court is permitted.

Key Requirement: Taxpayers must present recent (prior 12 months) comparable sales of similar properties in Maplewood. Real estate listings and tax comparisons with neighbors are not admissible evidence before the Board. All property taxes must be paid current to date to prosecute an appeal.
